Everyone else will be randomly put in three groups of four people to investigate money and to prepare a presentation on the topic that answers the following questions:

  1. What is money?
  2. Are there different forms of money?
  3. When did people first start using what we would call money?
  4. Is money a real thing? Why or why not?
  5. Do we need money? Why or why not?
  6. What are stablecoins?
  7. What is the current discussion in the U.S. around the use of stablecoins?
