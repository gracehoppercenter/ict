Tuesday, August 26th

Today we start with a chat about course objectives and class expectations. After that, we will have two stations to complete during class.

Station 1: Introduce Yourself to Me by Email

Using an email account which enables you to receive mail from the outside world, send me an email to my VCCS email address: jde232@email.vccs.edu introducing yourself to me by answering the following questions:

  1. What do you think entrepreneurship means? What do you think we will be doing in this class?
  2. What kinds of things do you like to do the most? What are your hobbies and passions? What activities bring you the most joy?
  3. What motivated you to sign up for this course, and what are you hoping to get out of it? Please be as specific as you can.
  4. Is there anything you would like to see in a Classroom Charter containing the rules and norms we all agree together to abide in our learning community?

Due: Email this assignment to me by the end of class so that I have time to reply before our class on Thursday.

Evaluation: An A will be earned for specific, detailed, and thoughful answers to my questions. B will be earned if all questions are answered, but specific details are lacking. C will be earned for answering most questions, and D will be earned for sending me an email.
