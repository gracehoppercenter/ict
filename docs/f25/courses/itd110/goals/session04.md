## What We'll Do In Class

### First - make a plan

By the end of class, you'll publish your first (very simple) website. Before we do that, we'll have a conversation about thoughtful, responsible web posting.

Here is a quick summary of what I plan to discuss in class:
- This class is about building websites, and I think the best way to learn is to actually build and publish real websites, but that **IS NOT A REQUIREMENT OF THIS CLASS**. You can get a 100% for both APS and NOVA without ever publishing a public website.
- Starting today, I'll give instructions that assume you want to publish a public website. Please let me know if you do not want to publish your work - I'll expect you to do all of the same work, but you'll publish it to a private location that only you and I share.
- Specifically I'll give instructions about building a portfolio website - where you'll write about your interests, skills, and projects. This is also **NOT A REQUIREMENT OF THIS CLASS**. You can get a 100% for both APS and NOVA without ever publishing anything about yourself to the internet. You are [required to develop a portfolio](https://virtualvirginia.instructure.com/courses/9233/pages/create-a-professional-electronic-portfolio?module_item_id=2907602), but not to publish it.
- As the year goes on, we will have ongoing conversations about comfort and responsibility in online publishing. One thing to start thinking about is whether you want to include a photo of yourself on your website. I have a photo of myself on my personal portfolio website, and many of my students choose to include a photo of themselves. You might choose the same, or you might not be comfortable doing so. We'll work through this consideration together.
