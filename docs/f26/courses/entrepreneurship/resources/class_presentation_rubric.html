Entrepreneurship Class Presentation Rubric

Grade Description
A

The topic presented is clearly and accurately summarized. There is evident understanding of the key idea(s), which are presented in a way that will help classmates remember them. The work shows evidence of effective use of the time allotted for its presentation, with care and pride of authorship on display in the presentation. Resources utilized are credited, with URLs included in the presentation. To earn credit, each presenter in a group must play an active role in the presentation.

All presentations should be submitted in PDF format for portability.

B The topic presented is clearly and accurately summarized with evident basic understanding of the key idea(s). Work lacks evidence of the full use of allotted time required for an A. Additional resources are not utilized and credited when they should have been.
C The topic is presented, though a clear understanding of the key idea(s) is lacking. No additional resources are are used. The quality of the work suggests that only a fraction of the allotted time was used in the effort.
D Some work was completed, but it shows only minimal effort and does not provide sufficient evidence of goal attainment or learning mastery.
E No evidence of work presented.
