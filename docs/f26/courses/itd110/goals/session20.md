## Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should:
- Be able to do simple addition and subtraction operations in binary
- Create and interpret binary representations of colors

### Today's Vocabulary:

- RGB
- Hexadecimal

## What We'll Do In Class

### No Quiz

### Programming Update

I've decided to move most of the binary math concepts to next semester - they will be easier to reason about and practice with once we have some JavaScript under our belts. Today we'll do some practice with addition and subtraction in binary, and for this unit I won't expect you to know any of the other math concepts that I introduced last time.

### Colors

We know how text and numbers are represented in binary - the last piece of 
information that we'll explore is photos. We'll do a short coloring activity in class to practice with this concept. We'll play with [this code.org color tool](https://studio.code.org/courses/pixelation/units/1/lessons/5/levels/1). This will get tedious quickly, so we'll introduce Hexadecimal ("hex") as
a shorthand that will make this much easier.

### Classwork - upload your completed color activity
