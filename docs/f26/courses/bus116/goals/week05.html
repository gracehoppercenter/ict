I did a bit more investigation into the Elk River DECA project, and found out that it has become well known since the students there started a business that grew beyond the boundries of the school campus. Specifically, they created:

  • The SWAGON: A trailer converted into a mobile retail store, allowing students to take their inventory directly to the public, appearing at community parades, local farmers' markets, and sporting events.
  • The SHOP Downtown: A holiday pop-up store located in downtown Elk River, introducing the enterprise to non-school-affiliated consumers.
  • E-Commerce Expansion: An integrated online store allows the student-run entity to process transactions, manage digital inventory, and fulfill orders outside of school operating hours.
  • The On-Campus Hub: The original brick-and-mortar storefront inside Elk River High School remains the operational command center and test market.

Teams We Need

  • Management team(organize business and make major decisions)
  • Marketing team(create advertisements and social media posts)
  • Finance team(keep track of money, prices, expenses, and profits)
  • Sales team(work with customers and sell products)
  • Inventory team(keep track of products and make sure everything is stocked and organized)
  • Technology/design team(create logos, graphics, and websites)

Each team would focus on a different part of the business and everyone would work together.

Business Ideas

  • Vending machine
  • Online merch store
  • Bakery
  • Services to other CTE businesses in our school
  • Online orders for 3D printed stuff
  • Create an auto shop store in collaboration with the auto tech program
  • An auction for unclaimed lost and found items
  • Form a math tutoring business to tutor elementary and middle school students
  • Partner with our animal science class to spend time with the animals, feed them, or dress them up
  • Sell garden accessories
