Teams We Need

  • Management team(organize business and make major decisions)
  • Marketing team(create advertisements and social media posts)
  • Finance team(keep track of money, prices, expenses, and profits)
  • Sales team(work with customers and sell products)
  • Inventory team(keep track of products and make sure everything is stocked and organized)
  • Technology/design team(create logos, graphics, and websites)

Each team would focus on a different part of the business and everyone would work together.

Business Ideas

  • Vending machine
  • Online merch store
  • Bakery
  • Services to other CTE businesses in our school
  • Online orders for 3D printed stuff
