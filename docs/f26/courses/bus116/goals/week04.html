BUS 116 Goals: Week 4

BUS 116

Week of Tuesday, September 22, 2026

Overview

This week we will begin the big project which will be a continuing focus of our course throughout the year, school-based enterprise (SBE). SBE will provide us with the practical, hands-on skills we want to learn to become entreprenuers.

Tuesday and Wednesday, September 22nd and 23rd

Class Goals / Tasks

Today we will investigate SBE and start planning for how we want to implement it in our class this year.

Read through the following websites:

  • DECA: School-Based Enterprise
  • Virginia Department of Education: CTE High-quality Work-based Learning (HQWBL)
  • Maryland Department of Education: School-Based Enterprises - Division of College and Career Pathways
  • DECA: Non-Traditional SBE's: Unique Locations
  • Goochland County Public Schools: Work-Based Learning

As you read through the websites, jot down some notes / thoughts on the following questions:

  1. What is school based enterprise? How would you describe it in your own words to a classmate at GHC in another course who asked you about it?
  2. What makes the Warren County and Elk River DECA SBEs non-traditional?
  3. What are the different skills we would need to have to successfully run SBEs at GHC?
  4. What kinds of teams should we organize so that we have different members of our class focusing on the skills you identified in the previous question available to our SBE project?
