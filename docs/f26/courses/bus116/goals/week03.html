The purpose of studying career clusters is to give you an opportunity to become more familiar with the different kinds of careers out their as you plot your path toward your future.

The specific framework for the clusters can vary. The U.S. Department of Education model lists 17 clusters, while the one we are using, which comes from Advance CTE has 14.
