Today we start with a chat about course objectives and class expectations. After that, we will have two stations to complete during class.

Assignment 1: Introduce Yourself

Using whatever word processing / text editing tool you like, create a PDF document introducing yourself to me by answering the following questions:

  1. Tell me a bit about yourself. How are you connected to GHC? Are you in Arlington Tech? Are you a CTE student from another school - if so, which one? What grade are you in? What are you planning to do when you graduate?
  2. What do you think entrepreneurship means? What do you think we will be doing in this class?
  3. What kinds of things do you like to do the most? What are your hobbies and passions? What activities bring you the most joy?
  4. Where do you see yourself five years from now? Where do you see yourself ten years from now?
  5. What kinds of things do you think you could do now to help you get to where you described yourself in the previous question?
  6. What motivated you to sign up for this course, and what are you hoping to get out of it? Please be as specific as you can.
  7. Is there anything you would like to see in a Classroom Charter containing the rules and norms we all agree together to abide in our learning community?

Evaluation: An A will be earned for specific, detailed, and thoughful answers to my questions submitted as a PDF file. B will be earned if all questions are answered, but specific details are lacking, or if you submitted the file in a format other than PDF. C will be earned for answering most questions, and D will be earned for submitting a PDF document.
