Entrepreneurship
This entrepreneurship class is designed for students who want to make a a positive impact on their community by starting their own worker cooperatives or non-profit organizations to provide goods and services or address local needs while also providing a decent and honest living for the co-op members or non-profit staff. Students will explore how to determine community needs, and devise a business plan around meeting an identified need. DE students will earn college credit through NVCC for BUS 116: Entrepreneurship.
Advanced Entrepreneurship
This course builds on the previous year by focusing on the skills and processes needed to implement the worker cooperative and non-profit business plans developed in the previous course. Students will learn how to incorporate a small business in Virginia, how to set up bookkeeping and payroll, how to establish tax exempt status with the IRS, and how governance works in the operation of democratically owned and operated businesses. DE students will earn college credit through NVCC for BUS 165: Small Business Management.

Let's start with something we definitely want to memorize:

ASSETS = LIABILITIES + OWNER'S EQUITY

Let's recall how our book introduces this equation with: What we have = Who owns it. In story in the book, the owner (the kid) starts with $5 of his own money and $10 of his mom's money. The $10 is a liability, since he has to pay it back to him mom. The $5 is owner's equity, since it is his own money. That gives him starting assets of $15.
