Entrepreneurship Work Submission Grading Rubric

Grade Description
A

Answers to all questions are in complete, gramatically correct sentences. The given question is clearly and accurately addressed. There is evident understanding in the answers of the key idea(s) about which the questions are asking. The work shows evidence of effective use of the time allotted for its preparation, with care and pride of authorship on display in the work. Group submissions list the names of all the submitters, and resources utilized are credited, with URLs included in the work submitted.

All work should be submitted in PDF format for portability.

B Answers to questions are in complete, mostly gramatically correct sentences. The given question is addressed. There is evidence in the answers of some understanding the key idea(s) about which the questions are asking. The work shows some lack of full use of the time allotted for it to warrant an A. The names of submitters are missing and additional resources are not utilized and credited when they should have been.
C The questions are answered, though a clear understanding of the key idea(s) is lacking. No additional resources are are used. The quality of the work suggests that only a fraction of the allotted time was used in the effort.
D Something was submitted, but it shows only minimal effort and does not provide sufficient evidence of goal attainment or learning mastery.
E Nothing submitted.
