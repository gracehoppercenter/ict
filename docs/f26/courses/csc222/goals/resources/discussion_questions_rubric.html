Discussion Questions Grading Rubric

Grade Description
A

The topic discussed is clearly and accurately summarized. There is evident understanding of the key idea(s), which are discussed in a way that will help remember them. The work shows clear evidence of effective use of the time allotted for its completion, with a commit history showing evidence of iterative development over this time. Additional resources are utilized when requested.

All discussion questions should be written in Markdown files under revision control in your git repository for this class.

B The topic discussed is clearly and accurately summarized with evident basic understanding of the key idea(s). Work lacks evidence of the full use of allotted time required for an A. Additional resources are not utilized when requested. Some responses to discussion questions are present, but they do not provide enough to illustrate all the key concepts.
C The topic is discussed, though a clear understanding if the key idea(s) is lacking. No additional resources are are used. At least one discussion question is not presented at all. Commit history suggests only a fraction of the allotted time was used in the effort.
D Some work was completed, but it shows only minimal effort and does not provide sufficient evidence of goal attainment or learning mastery.
E No evidence of work discussed.
