Monday, April 6th

Classwork

I met with Sara and Bella over spring break, and together we came up with a set of questions that will frame our investigation and the development of our business plan:

  1. What childcare resources already exist in Arlington?
  2. What licensing, training, and regulatory requirements do childcare providers face in Arlington?
  3. What resources, networks, and service providers do Arlington's childcare providers rely on? With which organizations do they affiliate?
  4. What challenges do childcare providers face in running their businesses?
  5. Which specific communities in Arlington do the current providers serve?
  6. Which communities are either not served or underserved by the currently existent childcare services? Which specific communities in Arlington do the current providers serve?
  7. What resources and models exist that can inspire us here in Arlington to address the needs identified in question 6?
  8. Can we develop a business plan that addresses the needs identified in question 6 guided by what we learned in questions 1 to 5 and inspired by what we learned in question 7?

We'll begin class today by looking over these questions and determining together if they indeed capture what we need to know to develop a good business plan.
