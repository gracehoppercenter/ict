  2. 26³ × 10³ = 17,576,000 license plates. We use the product principle because we're making an ordered selection where: 
    First position: 26 choices (any letter)
Second position: 26 choices (any letter)
Third position: 26 choices (any letter)
Fourth position: 10 choices (any digit)
Fifth position: 10 choices (any digit)
Sixth position: 10 choices (any digit)
    So we multiply: 26 × 26 × 26 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 17,576,000
  3. Rolling a die and flipping a coin: 6 × 2 = 12 outcomes (product principle) Rolling a die only if the coin shows heads: 1 (tails) + 6 (heads followed by die roll) = 7 outcomes (sum principle) The difference is that in the first scenario, both events always occur, while in the second, the die roll is conditional.
