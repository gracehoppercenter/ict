

## Project Management Tools

### Kanban Boards

Before we start class today, I'll introduce a new organization system that I've
setup for each of your projects in Codeberg.

First, we'll start with a history lesson. Much of modern business
comes from the mind of <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiichi_Ohno>, 
who invented tons of modern processes while working for Toyota in Japan. 
One of my favorite of Ohno's contributions is the ["5 whys"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_whys)
system for root-cause analysis. 

Several of these contributions are collectively called the
[Toyota Production System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Production_System)
and the one we'll use is called [Kanban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanban).

This process is almost a requirement of modern software development, and it'll
be the central component of our standups from now on.
