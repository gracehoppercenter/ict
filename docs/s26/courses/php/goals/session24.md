### Why CSS Text Styling Properties?

CSS offers a wide range of tools to control how HTML elements look.<br> Some properties and values are especially important for styling web pages.

In this section, our primary focus will be on text styling, encompassing font and text properties.<br> These elements play a pivotal role in defining the look of text on your web pages. <br>You'll gain proficiency in modifying font family, size, color, alignment, and various other attributes.

### What are List Properties?
List properties are valuable for both aesthetics and usability. Effective list styling not only reinforces your website's visual identity but also elevates readability, <br>facilitating content scanning and comprehension for users. Using consistent list styles across your entire <br>website will reinforce a sense of cohesion and refinement in the design.
